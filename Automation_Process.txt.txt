 Automation Process — IoT Project

1. Objective
The objective is to demonstrate how AI-generated research content can be converted into social media posts.

2. Input
The input is the IoT article saved in `04_Article/IoT_Article.txt`.

3. AI Tool Used
ChatGPT and gemini was used to help prepare the IoT article and five social media posts.

4. Process
1. Prepare an article about IoT and its connection with 5G.
2. Identify the main points from the article.
3. Convert the main points into five short social media posts.
4. Review each post for accuracy, clarity, and readability.
5. Save the final posts in `05_Social_Posts/IoT_Social_Posts.txt`.

5. Output
The output is a text file containing five social media posts about IoT.

6. Human Verification
The posts should be checked against reliable sources before being published. AI-generated content may contain mistakes, so human review is necessary.

7. Limitation
The content was created with AI assistance and reviewed manually. This is a simple workflow demonstration, not a fully automated software system.

Conclusion
This workflow shows how AI can help repurpose an article into social media content while keeping human verification as an important step.
