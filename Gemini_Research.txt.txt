# IoT Networks — Gemini Research Notes

1. IoT Architecture
An IoT system commonly includes four parts:
- **Perception layer:** Sensors and devices collect information.
- **Network layer:** Transfers data between devices and other systems.
- **Processing layer:** A server, cloud platform, or edge computer processes the data.
- **Application layer:** Presents information to users and supports useful services.

This is a simplified model; real IoT architectures can have different layers.

2. Communication Technologies
- **Wi-Fi:** Useful for devices that need local wireless connectivity and higher data rates.
- **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE):** Useful for short-range communication with low power consumption.
- **Zigbee:** Used in some low-power smart-home and sensor networks.
- **LoRaWAN:** Designed for low-power, long-range communication with relatively small amounts of data.
- **Cellular networks, including 4G and 5G:** Useful when devices need mobile-network coverage.

3. Examples of IoT Applications
- Smart agriculture uses sensors to monitor soil and support irrigation decisions.
- Industrial IoT monitors machinery and helps detect unusual operating conditions.
- Healthcare devices can collect and transmit health measurements.
- Smart-city systems can monitor traffic, air quality, and public infrastructure.
- Smart-home devices can support lighting, security, and energy management.

4. IoT and 5G

IoT is the system of connected devices and applications. 5G is a wireless communication technology that can provide connectivity for some IoT systems.
