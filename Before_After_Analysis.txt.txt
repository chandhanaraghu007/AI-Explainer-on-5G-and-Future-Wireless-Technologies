Before-and-After Analysis

1. Project Topic
AI Explainer on IoT Networks and 5G

2. Before Using AI Tools

Before using AI tools, the project required manual effort to understand IoT networks, organize technical information, prepare an article, create social media posts, and design a presentation.

- Information had to be collected and organized separately.
- Technical concepts such as IoT communication and 5G connectivity needed additional explanation.
- Creating multiple types of project content required more time.
- Checking the accuracy of technical claims was necessary.

3. After Using AI Tools

AI tools helped prepare and organize different parts of the project.

- ChatGPT helped explain IoT concepts and prepare research summaries, an article, and social media posts.
- Google Gemini provided a second AI-generated research perspective.
- AI image generation helped create educational visuals.
- AI-assisted content preparation helped organize the presentation.
- AI prompts helped plan the verification and article-to-social-post workflow.

4. Comparison

| Aspect | Before AI Assistance | After AI Assistance |
|---|---|---|
| Research | More manual effort | Faster initial information gathering |
| Understanding | Required individual study | Simple explanations supported learning |
| Article writing | Needed manual planning and drafting | AI-assisted drafting and organization |
| Social media posts | Needed separate preparation | Generated from the main topic |
| Visual content | Required separate design work | AI-assisted image creation |
| Verification | Needed source-by-source checking | Verification checklist helped organize checks |

5. Improvements Observed

AI tools helped save preparation time, organize information, generate ideas, and produce different content formats from the same topic.

6. Limitations Observed

AI-generated information may contain errors, incomplete explanations, or unsupported claims. Generated images may also contain incorrect labels or technical details. Reliable sources and human review are therefore necessary.

7. Conclusion

AI tools improved the speed and organization of this project. However, AI did not replace human understanding, source verification, or final review. The project combines AI assistance with human checking.

**Note:** This analysis describes the expected and observed workflow qualitatively. No exact time savings or accuracy improvements were measured.
